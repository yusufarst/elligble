## 4. Recovery/rollback decision rules and target

Recovery target fields **UNKNOWN**: previous validated image digest/config, exact known schema history, encrypted DB backup/WAL or other approved recovery mechanism, pinned media manifest, approved RPO/RTO, eligible accepted-write interval, operating approver/operator and incident contact. The runbook's migrations are forward-only; old images reject unknown migrations. Do not claim any previous image can boot after a schema change or that restoring an old backup preserves later answers.

Decision triggers for a future authorized release: startup/migration failure, critical route/auth/tenant breach, answer/score/timer/session inconsistency, impossible recovery or missing media, operational/security/backup failure, or approved capacity/error budget breach. Stop traffic switch/escalation and preserve evidence; do not continue at reduced test scope. During active exams, the runbook prefers safe roll-forward over older restore that loses accepted writes; any emergency action still needs explicit authority and audited procedure.

- **Code-only, unchanged compatible schema:** rollback to the rehearsed prior immutable image/config only when migration checks, pending client/answer compatibility and candidate data semantics are validated. Record reason/time/actor/outcome and scoped synthetic checks/telemetry after recovery. No silent rollback of accepted business state.
- **Schema changed/unknown migration history:** current runner may prevent previous image startup. Prefer separately authorized compatible roll-forward fix when safe. A pre-release restore is an exceptional operating decision only after P9 RT protocol shows recovery point, media/schema consistency and post-backup accepted-write accounting. No destructive down migration, check bypass or ordinary coding-agent manual SQL.
- **Acknowledged answers after backup:** preserve original DB/log/WAL/receipt/intent evidence and account for every accepted write and subsequent authorized change. If the proposed target loses accepted answers or consistency is unproven, mark FAIL/NO-GO and escalate; never label restored old counts a Zero-Lost-Answer PASS. Actual recovery mechanism and RPO/RTO remain Owner/ops decisions, not invented promises.
- **Verification after recovery:** exact image/schema/config/restore point, tenant/auth/session ownership, same Attempt/snapshot/answer versions/time/submission/frozen results, authorized media, no new duplicate account/Attempt/submit, logs/alerts and P8 scoped regression. Broader release gates remain unmet until failures are resolved and reviewed; no automatic CI rerun or deployment loop to erase failure history.

This section specifies evidence and decisions only. No production command, process/container stop/restart, restore, access/config change, deployment or merge is executed/authorized here. Isolation includes MULTIPLECORP, BANTUKASIR and PRANATA UNY on the Owner workstation; future GitHub/staging runners are separate resources with their own explicit scope.
