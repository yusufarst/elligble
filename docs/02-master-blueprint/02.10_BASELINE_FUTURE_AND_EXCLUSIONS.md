## 1. Purpose

It consolidates canonical classifications verified against the repository's root product vision (D01.1).

## 4. Exclusions and Constraints

### 4.1. Optional Capabilities
The following capability is canonically distinct from the mandatory baseline:
- **Learn**: `OPTIONAL`

### 4.2. Explicit Exclusions (Out of Baseline / Future)
The following capabilities are explicitly declared as NOT part of the initial baseline:
- AI-dependent functionality (Predictive Failure AI, AI Proctoring, AI Analytics).
- Pre-enrollment or admission functionality.
- Social media/community network functionality (Parent Social Community, Alumni Social Network).
- Continuous Video Surveillance (not default/baseline).
- Offline learning session tracking, Bell/JP scheduling, General PBM timetable.
- Full electronic report card (E-Rapor) or SIS/ERP replacements.

### 4.3. Production Blockers (Readiness Constraints)
Regardless of the maturity state of associated capabilities (OPEN or PROVISIONAL), there are 12 explicit Production Blockers that must be fully resolved prior to launch. Their blocking scope varies by item, applying either system-wide or conditionally based on the capabilities being launched (such as Care and Partner):

1. Controller / Processor Legal Allocation
2. Final Retention Periods / Retention Matrix
3. Required DPIA
4. Full Authentication Policy / Security Policy
5. Permission Matrix
6. Assessment Capability Testing
7. Zero-Lost-Answer Verification
8. Care Safeguarding Policy (where Care launches)
9. Partner Verification / Moderation Policy (where Partner launches)
10. Data Classification + Consent Governance
11. Backup + Restore Verification
12. Security / Incident-Response Readiness
