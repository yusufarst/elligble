## 1. Purpose

The Traceability Matrix establishes auditable correspondence between upstream authority (Discovery, Recovery, and Governance) and Master Blueprint coverage. It guarantees that material capability, boundary, and domain definition mapped in the Master Blueprint derives from an authorized canonical decision through either a DIRECT TRACE or an AUTHORIZED SYNTHESIS. No material Blueprint claim may exist without identifiable upstream authority. MB-11 records traceability; it does not change the traced decisions.

## 2. Authority & Traceability Rules

- **Repository Source is Absolute:** Traceability citations must point directly to repository source files. Antigravity reports or AI summaries are not canonical sources.
- **Supersession Rules:** 
  - newer alone does not supersede;
  - older alone does not supersede;
  - LOCKED/FROZEN remains binding unless explicit canonical supersession exists;
  - authority/status/supersession determine precedence;
  - true same-axis unresolved conflict requires Owner decision.
- **Bidirectional Verification:** Every upstream item must map forward into the Blueprint; every Blueprint claim must map backward to an upstream source.
