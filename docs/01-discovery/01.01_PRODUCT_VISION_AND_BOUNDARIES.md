# D01.2A — Strategic Addendum: ELLIGBLE Talent Assurance

**Strategic Direction:** APPROVED  
**Product Mechanism Status:** FUTURE / PROVISIONAL  
**Legal / Commercial Mechanism:** REQUIRES DEDICATED DISCOVERY  
**Baseline Build:** DO NOT IMPLEMENT GUARANTEE MECHANISM YET

## Strategic Purpose

ELLIGBLE Talent Assurance is intended to become a differentiated trust layer that helps partners evaluate candidates using evidence-backed records rather than relying only on self-declared CV/profile information.

It may combine, where legitimately available:

```text
Passport provenance
verified academic/development records
verified credentials
assessment results
assessment integrity context
explicit eligibility criteria
readiness evidence
application-specific evidence
issuer/verifier identity
audit trail
```

## Assurance Boundary

ELLIGBLE Talent Assurance does **not** guarantee:

```text
candidate will be hired
candidate will pass an interview
candidate will perform successfully after hiring
candidate will remain employed
candidate will be admitted to a university
candidate will obtain a scholarship
```

The final selection decision remains with the relevant partner.

## What ELLIGBLE May Eventually Assure

### Layer A — Record Provenance Assurance

Potential assurance:

```text
where the record came from
who issued it
whether evidence exists
whether ELLIGBLE verified it
verification status
verification timestamp
record version/history
```

This is the safest and strongest initial Talent Assurance layer.

### Layer B — Assessment Integrity Assurance

Potential assurance:

```text
assessment identity
assessment issuer
methodology/version
candidate result
exam security conditions
verification status
```

ELLIGBLE can attest what the system actually measured and how the result was produced, but should not overstate what the assessment predicts.

### Layer C — Criteria / Eligibility Assurance

Potential assurance:

> At a specific timestamp, based on available verified data, the candidate satisfied a defined set of objective criteria.

Examples:

```text
minimum certificate exists
required assessment threshold met
required academic condition met
required skill evidence present
required document verified
```

This is stronger than generic matching but remains criteria-based.

### Layer D — Readiness Assurance

This is higher risk.

ELLIGBLE may eventually state that a candidate meets an explicitly defined **ELLIGBLE Readiness Standard** or partner-defined readiness framework, provided:

```text
the standard is transparent
the dimensions are explainable
the methodology is validated
evidence freshness is known
limitations are disclosed
no hidden high-impact automated decision is used
```

Do not equate readiness with guaranteed job performance.

### Layer E — Conditional Commercial Assurance

Potential future models may include:

```text
replacement candidate
service credit
re-verification
fee adjustment
limited contractual remedy
```

only when a verified mismatch is demonstrably attributable to an ELLIGBLE verification failure or a precisely defined assurance condition.

This layer is NOT approved for baseline product implementation.

## Important Wording Correction

The phrase:

> "Kesesuaian kandidat terhadap standar kesiapan posisi tertentu dijamin."

is too broad if used without a defined standard and verification methodology.

Preferred future wording:

> **ELLIGBLE dapat mengesahkan bahwa, pada waktu verifikasi, kandidat memenuhi kriteria atau standar kesiapan yang didefinisikan secara eksplisit berdasarkan bukti yang tersedia dan terverifikasi.**

This distinguishes:

```text
verified criteria satisfaction
≠
future performance guarantee
```

## Liability Boundary

ELLIGBLE may take responsibility for the **verification statement that ELLIGBLE itself issues**, within a clearly defined scope.

It should not automatically accept responsibility for:

```text
false information from external issuers beyond reasonable verification scope
post-verification changes
partner hiring decisions
candidate behavior after selection
future performance
external events
outdated evidence outside defined freshness rules
```

Exact contractual liability, claims, remedies, fees, exclusions, insurance/financial implications, and dispute handling require dedicated Legal + Commercial Discovery before launch.

## Strategic Differentiation

Recommended positioning:

```text
Ordinary profile platform
→ "Candidate says this about themselves."

ELLIGBLE Passport
→ "This record has provenance, evidence, and verification status."

ELLIGBLE Talent Assurance
→ "For this defined purpose, ELLIGBLE can attest which verified facts/criteria are supported by evidence."
```

This should become a strategic differentiator only after the underlying Passport, Trust, Assessment, Partner, and Outcome foundations are reliable.

## Sequencing

Recommended dependency order:

```text
Identity
↓
Academic / Assessment Truth
↓
Passport Provenance
↓
Verification Workflows
↓
Partner Applications
↓
Outcome Data
↓
Talent Assurance Discovery
↓
Validated Assurance Standard
↓
Commercial / Legal Model
↓
Pilot
```

Do not build Talent Assurance before its evidence foundation exists.

```text
Weak fit / major scope drift
```

# D01.4 — Product Principles & Non-Negotiables

**Review Status:** REVIEWED & LOCKED  
**Owner Approval:** APPROVED — OVERALL AGREEMENT

## Purpose

Define the product rules that must remain true even as features, UI, technical stack, pricing, and integrations evolve.

These principles are intended to prevent ELLIGBLE from becoming inconsistent, unsafe, over-engineered, commercially distorted, or difficult for schools to operate.

---

## D01.4-01 — Student Journey Is the Product Center

ELLIGBLE should be designed around one continuous student journey, not around disconnected feature silos.

```text
SCHOOL
↓
LEARN
↓
ASSESS
↓
TRACK
↓
SUPPORT
↓
PASSPORT
↓
PATH
↓
OPPORTUNITY
↓
OUTCOME
↓
ALUMNI
```

Role-specific experiences should support the same journey.

**Non-negotiable:** a module may not optimize its local workflow in a way that breaks the continuity, trust, or privacy of the broader student journey.

**Recommendation:** LOCK.

---

## D01.4-02 — Secure Assessment Has Mission-Critical Priority

When active assessment reliability conflicts with noncritical platform functionality:

```text
ANSWER PERSISTENCE
TIMER AUTHORITY
SUBMISSION
EXAM SECURITY
RECOVERY
```

take priority over:

```text
search
messaging
analytics
partner features
billing
noncritical notifications
```

**Non-negotiable:** no unrelated module may make an active exam fail.

**Recommendation:** LOCK.

---

## D01.4-03 — Zero-Lost-Answer Is a Product Promise

ELLIGBLE must be engineered so that ordinary failures such as:

```text
network interruption
refresh
browser crash
temporary disconnect
approved device transfer
server retry
double submit
```

do not silently destroy student answers.

No implementation may trade answer safety for development convenience.

**Recommendation:** LOCK.

---

## D01.4-04 — Security and Privacy Are Default Architecture, Not Add-Ons

Security/privacy must exist from the first relevant Build Unit.

Required mindset:

```text
least privilege
tenant isolation
purpose limitation
minimum necessary data
audit
safe defaults
sensitive-data separation
secure recovery
revocable access
```

Do not postpone these as a “hardening phase” after functionality is built.

**Recommendation:** LOCK.

---

## D01.4-05 — One Human, One Long-Lived Identity Where Possible

ELLIGBLE should avoid creating duplicate identities merely because a person:

```text

graduates
becomes alumni
becomes guardian
becomes staff
joins a partner organization
```

Lifecycle/context changes should generally occur through membership/assignment, not duplicate Person records.

**Recommendation:** LOCK.

# D01.6A — Secure Assessment Addendum: Proctor Incident & Violation Feed

**Review Status:** REVIEWED & LOCKED  
**Owner Approval:** APPROVED — OWNER REQUIREMENT

## Requirement

Every proctor must have an operational realtime **Feed Kejadian/Pelanggaran** for the exam scope assigned to that proctor.

This is not a social feed.

The feed exists to help the proctor understand what is happening during a live exam and decide what operational action is needed.

## Scope Rule

A proctor may only receive feed events for:

```text
assigned exam
assigned exam room
assigned participant set
or another explicitly authorized monitoring scope
```

A proctor must not automatically see incidents from unrelated rooms, exams, or tenants.

## Minimum Event Content

Each feed item should eventually be able to show:

```text
timestamp
participant
exam / room context
event type
severity / priority
short event summary
signal source
current review status
save / network / device context when relevant
evidence availability indicator
recommended/available action
```

## Candidate Event Types

Examples:

```text
FOCUS_LOST
TAB_SWITCHED
FULLSCREEN_EXITED
MULTI_WINDOW_SUSPECTED
MULTI_WINDOW_DETECTED
PICTURE_IN_PICTURE_DETECTED
WINDOW_NOT_FULL_SCREEN
COPY_ATTEMPT
PASTE_ATTEMPT
CUT_ATTEMPT
CONTEXT_MENU_ATTEMPT
DEVTOOLS_SIGNAL
SECOND_SESSION_DETECTED
DEVICE_CHANGE_REQUESTED
NETWORK_UNSTABLE
OFFLINE
SAVE_RETRYING
UNSYNCED_STATE
CAMERA_UNAVAILABLE
SCREENSHOT_EVIDENCE_CAPTURED
LOCATION_CONTEXT_CHANGED
BATTERY_LOW
MANUAL_WARNING_ISSUED
PARTICIPANT_LOCKED
PARTICIPANT_UNLOCKED
DEVICE_TRANSFER_APPROVED
EXAM_TERMINATED
SUBMISSION_FAILED
SUBMISSION_RECOVERED
```

Exact canonical event taxonomy belongs in Secure Assessment Discovery.

## Feed Behavior

Recommended baseline:

```text
newest event visible quickly
time-ordered history retained
severity/filter controls
room/participant filters
unreviewed indicator
acknowledge/review state
resolved/dismissed state where appropriate
deduplication / event grouping
participant drill-down
link to permitted evidence
```

Repeated low-level signals should be grouped where possible so the feed does not become unusable during a large exam.

Example:

```text
BAD:
Student A changed focus 17 times
→ 17 identical feed cards

BETTER:
Student A — repeated focus-loss signals
17 events in 4 minutes
→ Open Timeline
```

## Severity Principle

A feed item should distinguish technical operational problems from integrity signals.

Candidate dimensions:

```text
INFO
ATTENTION
HIGH
CRITICAL
```

Severity is not the same thing as a cheating verdict.

Examples:

```text
LOW BATTERY
→ operational warning

UNSYNCED ANSWERS
→ potentially critical reliability issue

MULTI_WINDOW_SUSPECTED
→ integrity signal requiring review

SECOND ACTIVE SESSION CONFIRMED
→ deterministic technical policy event
```

## Signal ≠ Verdict

Canonical rule remains:

```text
EVENT
≠
INCIDENT
≠
CHEATING DECISION
```

The feed helps the proctor review and respond; it does not automatically label the student as cheating.

## Evidence Privacy

The main feed should not unnecessarily display full sensitive evidence.

Preferred:

```text
feed summary
→ evidence available
→ authorized drill-down
```

Screenshot/camera evidence access must respect assessment policy, privacy, role scope, and retention requirements.

## Reliability

The feed is operational monitoring, but answer persistence must not depend on it.

```text
FEED REALTIME FAILURE
must not cause
ANSWER SAVE FAILURE
```

Feed/realtime delivery may degrade while the exam persistence path continues independently.

## Future UX Direction

Recommended desktop proctor workspace:

```text
Participant Grid / Cards
+
Incident & Violation Feed
+
Selected Participant Detail / Timeline
+
Critical Alerts
```

The feed should support rapid scanning during large multi-room exams.
