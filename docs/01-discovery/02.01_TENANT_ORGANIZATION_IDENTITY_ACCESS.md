# ELLIGBLE — Discovery 02: Tenant, Organization, Identity & Access Foundation

## Purpose

Define the identity and access model that every later ELLIGBLE domain depends on.

This Discovery must establish clear distinctions between:

```text
Person
User Account
Organization
Tenant
Organization Group
Membership
Role / Base Access Type
Assignment
Capability
Scope
Policy / Context
```

It must support multi-role, multi-organization, transfer, graduation, guardian relationships, partner memberships, and governed platform access without creating duplicate identities or omnipotent superusers.

# D02.1 — Canonical Identity & Organization Mental Model

**Review Status:** REVIEWED & LOCKED  
**Owner Approval:** APPROVED — OVERALL AGREEMENT

## D02.1-01 — Person ≠ User Account

Recommended canonical model:

```text
PERSON
= the real human identity represented in ELLIGBLE

USER ACCOUNT
= the credential/login mechanism used to access ELLIGBLE
```

A Person should ideally remain stable throughout the lifecycle.

Examples:

```text
Person: Andi
↓
student at School A
↓
transfers to School B
↓
graduates
↓
alumni
↓
becomes mentor
```

This should not create a new Person each time.

A User Account may be:

```text
active
disabled
recovered
locked
merged
replaced
```

without deleting the underlying Person history.

**Recommendation:** LOCK.

---

## D02.1-02 — Person ≠ Membership

Membership means a Person belongs to an Organization within a defined context/time.

```text
Person
↓
Membership
↓
Organization
```

Examples:

```text
Andi
→ student membership at SMA A

Bu Sari
→ staff membership at SMA A

Pak Budi
→ guardian relationship to Andi
→ may not be school staff

Dewi
→ partner-staff membership at Company X
```

A Person may have multiple memberships over time or concurrently where legitimate.

**Recommendation:** LOCK.

---

## D02.1-03 — Organization ≠ Tenant

Recommended distinction:

```text
ORGANIZATION
= a real-world institutional entity represented in ELLIGBLE

TENANT
= an operational isolation boundary for an academic school environment
```

For current academic scope:

```text
one school
≈
one academic tenant
```

but the concepts must remain separate.

Example:

```text
Organization:
SMA Negeri 1 Mlati

Academic Tenant:
SMA Negeri 1 Mlati operational data boundary
```

A Partner Organization can exist without becoming an Academic Tenant.

**Recommendation:** LOCK.

---

## D02.1-04 — Organization Group ≠ Tenant

An Organization Group represents a relationship between organizations, for example:

```text
foundation
school network
education group
government-managed cluster
```

Example:

```text
Yayasan ABC
├── SMA ABC 1
├── SMK ABC
└── MA ABC
```

Each school remains its own academic tenant unless explicitly designed otherwise.

Group-level authority must not automatically bypass tenant isolation.

**Recommendation:** LOCK.

---

## D02.1-05 — Academic Tenant Scope

Current academic tenant types:

```text
SMA
SMK
MA
MAK
sederajat yang explicitly supported
```

Current excluded tenant scope:

```text
SMP / MTs
```

Partner organizations, universities, companies, training providers, scholarship providers, merchants, and other opportunity providers are not academic tenants.

**Recommendation:** LOCK.

---

## D02.1-06 — Membership Is Time-Bounded

**Recommendation:** LOCK principle.

## D02.1-07 — Base Access Type ≠ Assignment

```text
PLATFORM_OWNER
PLATFORM_STAFF
SCHOOL_STAFF
STUDENT
GUARDIAN
ALUMNI

```

Assignments provide operational responsibility.

Examples:

```text
SCHOOL_STAFF
+ TEACHER

SCHOOL_STAFF
+ HOMEROOM_TEACHER

SCHOOL_STAFF
+ PROCTOR

SCHOOL_STAFF
+ PRINCIPAL
```

One Person may hold multiple assignments.

**Recommendation:** LOCK.

---

## D02.1-08 — Assignment Must Be Scoped

An Assignment should not automatically apply to the entire tenant.

Examples:

```text
TEACHER
scope:

PROCTOR
scope:

HOMEROOM_TEACHER
scope:
Class XB / Academic Year 2026

COUNSELOR

```

**Recommendation:** LOCK.

---

## D02.1-09 — Authorization Formula

Recommended conceptual authorization model:

```text
AUTHORIZATION
=
BASE ACCESS
+
MEMBERSHIP
+
ASSIGNMENT
+
CAPABILITY
+
SCOPE
+
POLICY / CONTEXT
```

Not merely:

```text

```

This hybrid model provides the flexibility needed for real schools without creating one-off permissions for every user.

**Recommendation:** LOCK.

---

## D02.1-10 — Capability ≠ Role

Role/assignment describes responsibility.

Capability describes permitted action.

Example:

```text

VIEW_ASSIGNED_INCIDENT_FEED
ISSUE_WARNING
LOCK_PARTICIPANT
REQUEST/APPROVE_DEVICE_TRANSFER
```

Exact capability naming belongs to later permission-matrix work.

**Recommendation:** LOCK.

## D02.1-12 — Contextual Policy Can Further Restrict Access

Examples:

**Recommendation:** LOCK.

**Recommendation:** LOCK.

## D02.1-14 — Multi-Organization Is Valid

A Person may legitimately belong to multiple organizations.

Examples:

```text

+

Platform Staff
```

Authorization must be evaluated within the selected/active organization context.

**Recommendation:** LOCK.

## D02.1-15 — Transfer Preserves Identity

Student transfer should normally behave like:

```text

↓
old school membership ENDED
↓

```

The new school must not automatically receive unrestricted access to all historical data from the prior school.

Cross-school continuity requires explicit data-sharing rules later.

**Recommendation:** LOCK.

---

## D02.1-16 — Graduation Preserves Identity

Graduation:

```text

↓

↓

↓

```

Graduation must not create a new alumni account if the same Person already exists.

**Recommendation:** LOCK.

---

## D02.1-17 — Guardian Is Relationship-Based

Guardian access should derive from verified relationship to a student, not from generic school membership.

Conceptually:

```text

↓
Guardian Relationship
↓
Student Person
```

A guardian may have multiple children across different school tenants.

**Recommendation:** LOCK.

# D02.2 — Organization, Tenant & Group Lifecycle

**Review Status:** REVIEWED & LOCKED  
**Owner Approval:** APPROVED — OVERALL AGREEMENT

## Purpose

Define how organizations and academic tenants are created, verified, activated, suspended, grouped, closed, and historically preserved without breaking tenant isolation or student continuity.

---

## D02.2-01 — Organization Is the Institutional Record

An Organization represents the institutional entity itself.

Examples:

```text
SMA Negeri 1 Mlati
SMK ABC
MA XYZ
Universitas ABC
PT XYZ
Yayasan Pendidikan ABC
```

The Organization record should hold stable institutional identity and metadata, while operational school isolation is handled by Tenant.

**Recommendation:** LOCK.

---

## D02.2-02 — Academic Tenant Is Created Only for Supported School Organizations

Current academic tenant scope:

```text
SMA
SMK
MA
MAK
supported equivalent upper-secondary schools
```

Creating an Organization does not automatically create an Academic Tenant.

Example:

```text
University ABC
→ Organization
→ no Academic Tenant

Company XYZ
→ Partner Organization
→ no Academic Tenant
```

**Recommendation:** LOCK.

---

## D02.2-03 — One School = One Academic Tenant Baseline

Recommended baseline:

```text
ONE SCHOOL ORGANIZATION
=
ONE ACADEMIC TENANT
```

This keeps:

```text
academic data
users/memberships
assessment
Care
Track
school configuration
```

isolated per school.

Exceptions should require explicit future design rather than ad hoc sharing.

**Recommendation:** LOCK.

---

## D02.2-04 — Tenant ID Is Immutable

Each Academic Tenant must have an immutable internal identifier.

Human-readable fields such as:

```text
school name
tenant code
NPSN
slug
domain
branding
```

may change.

Internal tenant identity must not.

**Recommendation:** LOCK.

---

## D02.2-05 — Human-Readable Tenant Code Is Separate From Tenant ID

Recommended:

```text
tenant_id
= immutable internal identifier

tenant_code
= controlled human-readable identifier
```

Example:

```text
tenant_id:
01H...

tenant_code:
SMAN1MLATI
```

Tenant code may support onboarding/admin UX but must not be used as the primary security boundary.

**Recommendation:** LOCK.

---

## D02.2-06 — NPSN Is a Reference, Not the Internal Tenant Key

Where relevant:

```text
NPSN
= external/official institutional reference
```

It must not become the canonical internal Tenant ID.

Reasons include:

```text
data correction
institutional changes
non-NPSN organizations
future integration variability
```

**Recommendation:** LOCK.

---

## D02.2-07 — Organization Verification Is Distinct From Tenant Activation

Recommended lifecycle distinction:

```text
Organization Verification
≠
Tenant Activation
```

An institution may be verified as legitimate but not yet ready to operate ELLIGBLE.

Potential Organization verification states:

```text
UNVERIFIED
PENDING_VERIFICATION
VERIFIED
REJECTED
SUSPENDED
```

Exact vocabulary may be refined later.

**Recommendation:** LOCK principle.

---

## D02.2-08 — Tenant Lifecycle Must Be Explicit

Candidate Academic Tenant lifecycle:

```text
DRAFT
ONBOARDING
ACTIVE
SUSPENDED
CLOSING
CLOSED
```

Meaning:

```text
DRAFT
→ created but not yet onboarding

ONBOARDING
→ configuration/import/setup in progress

ACTIVE
→ operational

SUSPENDED
→ access or selected operations restricted

CLOSING
→ controlled shutdown/export/retention process

CLOSED
→ no normal operations, history retained per policy
```

Exact state names may be refined, but lifecycle separation is mandatory.

**Recommendation:** LOCK principle.

---

## D02.2-09 — Suspension Must Not Destroy Data

Tenant suspension may restrict access or selected operations, but must not:

```text
delete student history
erase Assessment results
erase Passport provenance
destroy audit logs
```

Suspension and deletion are different concepts.

**Recommendation:** LOCK.

---

## D02.2-10 — Active Exams Must Be Protected From Commercial Suspension

If a tenant enters billing/commercial restriction:

```text
ACTIVE EXAM
must not be abruptly terminated
```

Commercial enforcement must occur at a safe boundary.

Example:

```text
block creation of new exam
or
block next billing-cycle activation
```

rather than corrupting an already running exam.

**Recommendation:** LOCK.

---

## D02.2-11 — Closing a Tenant Requires Controlled Exit

Tenant closure should include later-defined workflow for:

```text
data export
active-user notification
finalization of active workflows
retention policy application
credential/access shutdown
pending support cases
partner/application continuity where applicable
audit preservation
```

**Recommendation:** LOCK.

---

## D02.2-12 — Closed Tenant History Must Remain Addressable

A closed school tenant may still be referenced historically by:

```text
alumni Passport provenance
academic records
assessment history
verified credentials
audit
outcome reporting
```

Closing a tenant must not make valid historical records orphaned.

**Recommendation:** LOCK.

---

## D02.2-13 — Organization Rename Must Preserve History

If a school changes name:

```text

→ historical metadata

new name
→ current display name
```

Do not create a new Organization merely because the display name changes.

**Recommendation:** LOCK.

## D02.2-14 — Institutional Merge/Split Is Not a Simple Rename

If institutions:


**Recommendation:** LOCK.

**Recommendation:** LOCK.

## D02.2-16 — Group Membership Is Time-Bounded

A school may join or leave a group.


**Recommendation:** LOCK.

## D02.2-17 — Group-Level Access Must Be Explicitly Granted

# D02.10-A — LOCKED Canonical Foundation

## Identity Model

```text
Person ≠ User Account ≠ Membership
```

- One human should ideally map to one long-lived Person.
- Person may exist before account activation.
- Account lifecycle does not erase Person history.

- Partner Staff is a Person with Partner Organization membership.

- Weak similarity never silently merges Persons.

- Organization Groups may relate multiple institutions without bypassing tenant isolation.
- `tenant_id` is immutable.
- Human-readable tenant codes/NPSN/names/branding are not security boundaries.
- Tenant lifecycle is explicit and non-destructive.
- Closing/suspending tenants does not erase valid history.
- Cross-tenant access is never implicit.

## Privilege Governance

- No self-escalation.
- Grant authority is separately authorized and scoped.
- Sensitive access may require step-up, approval, expiry, reason, or maker-checker.
- Maker and checker are distinct where maker-checker applies.
- Temporary privilege auto-expires.
- Delegation is governed and is not credential sharing.
- Delegated authority cannot exceed its valid authority basis.
- Offboarding ends memberships/assignments and revokes relevant sessions.
- Sensitive access requires periodic review capability.
- Orphaned tenant administration requires controlled institutional recovery.
- No permanent “temporary admin”.

**Status:** LOCKED.

## Authentication & Recovery

Baseline direction:

```text
ELLIGBLE ID / username
+
password
```

- Student email is not mandatory.
- Student phone is not mandatory.
- Minimum password length = 8 characters.
- No mandatory periodic password rotation by default.
- MFA/strong step-up is risk-based and required for privileged contexts.
- Recovery is layered.
- School-assisted student recovery is supported.
- Staff/support never view passwords.
- Support never requests password, OTP, raw token, or recovery secret.
- Temporary credentials are short-lived/activation-only.
- Authentication secrets never appear in logs/audit.
- Provider auth remains replaceable.

**Status:** LOCKED.

## Session Model

- General ELLIGBLE sessions may be multi-device.
- Exam Session is separate and may enforce one active exam session per attempt.
- Sessions must be revocable.
- Revocation must take effect promptly for high-risk access.
- Remembered device is convenience, not permanent identity proof.
- Device/browser context is useful but fallible.
- Ordinary session expiry must not destroy active Assessment attempt state.
- Authentication outage during active exams requires an Assessment-safe recovery strategy.

**Status:** LOCKED.

## Guardian & Multi-Tenant Model

- One guardian may have multiple children across multiple school tenants.
- Guardian relationship may persist, but projections remain tenant/policy aware.
- Guardian access does not inherit student capabilities.
- Multi-tenant users explicitly switch context.
- Permission/navigation/search/cache context is re-evaluated after tenant switch.
- Staff authority in one tenant never transfers automatically to another.
- Global Person continuity never implies global operational-data access.

**Status:** LOCKED.

## Provisioning & Import

Canonical process:

```text
SOURCE
↓
VALIDATE
↓
IDENTITY RESOLUTION
↓
PREVIEW / REVIEW
↓
CONFIRM
↓
PERSON (if truly new)
↓
MEMBERSHIP
↓
ASSIGNMENT (if authorized)
↓
ACCOUNT PROVISIONING
↓
ACTIVATION
↓
AUDIT + CORRECTION
```

- Bulk CSV/Excel import is first-class.
- No direct blind production writes from upload.
- Import has preview/dry-run.
- Import is idempotent.
- Errors are row/field actionable.
- Bad rows need not invalidate all valid rows.
- Raw import files are sensitive.
- No permanent password spreadsheets.
- Printable single-use activation sheets are allowed as a school-friendly direction.
- Identity conflict review queue is required.
- Import never silently merges Persons.
- School imports can never create Platform Owner/Platform Staff.
- Duplicate matching baseline works without paid AI.
- Spreadsheet/file security is an implementation requirement.

**Status:** LOCKED.

## Audit & Accountability

- Sensitive audit is append-oriented.
- Audit captures actor/context/target/action/time and reason where needed.
- High-impact changes preserve before/after or equivalent trustworthy diff.
- Audit does not store secrets.
- Audit minimizes sensitive payload.
- Audit access and export are separately permissioned.
- Server time is authoritative.
- Background jobs preserve initiator and execution provenance.
- Service identities are not human admin identities.
- Break-glass produces high-severity audit.

**Status:** LOCKED.

```text
7. notify where appropriate
8. repair without silently rewriting history
```

Default sensitive authorization failure:

```text
FAIL SAFE / DENY
```

Missing scope/tenant never means global.
