# ELLIGBLE — Discovery 04: Secure Assessment

## Purpose

Define Secure Assessment as ELLIGBLE's highest-priority flagship domain: a trusted, resilient, high-stakes-capable assessment system that can operate safely in real school conditions without destabilizing active exams, losing student answers, overclaiming anti-cheating certainty, or conflating academic structure with exam runtime.

## D04.1-21 — ARCHIVED Preserves History and Enables Controlled Delete

Archived exams remain queryable/auditable under retention policy and preserve required provenance.

ARCHIVED is also the **only normal lifecycle state from which an owner-authorized delete/purge action may become available**.

Canonical distinction:

```text
ARCHIVED
= lifecycle state

DELETE / PURGE
= controlled destructive action after archive
```

Deletion is therefore **not another ordinary exam lifecycle status**.

Baseline requirements for `Delete After Archive`:

```text
- exam must already be ARCHIVED
- authorized capability required
- destructive confirmation required
- dependency/impact check required
- retention / legal hold / audit constraints checked
- active attempts/sessions must not exist
- finalized official projections or downstream references must be evaluated
- action is audited
```

Where policy requires preservation, ELLIGBLE may remove operational content while retaining a minimal non-sensitive tombstone/provenance record rather than pretending the exam never existed.

Exact hard-delete vs purge/tombstone rules remain later retention/privacy/architecture work.

**Recommendation:** LOCK.
