## 4. CLOSURE RULES
1. A blocker remains OPEN until evidence exists.
2. Carry-forward approval != resolution.
3. Architecture design != production readiness evidence.
4. Build completion alone != automatic blocker closure.
5. Closure requires evidence appropriate to the blocker.
6. Conditional Care / Partner blockers activate according to their canonical launch scope.
7. Any maturity/scope change must come from proper canonical authority.
8. Closing a blocker requires updating this backlog and any applicable state/manifest/governance record in a controlled execution.
9. No blocker may be silently deleted from the backlog.
10. Production must not proceed through an applicable blocker gate while that blocker remains unresolved.
